# New Games

When you play something LilyPad can't match to a game in your FrogLog, it doesn't throw the time away. It records the sessions anyway and lists the game under **New Games**, for you to sort out when it suits you.

LilyPad notices:

- **Steam games** that aren't in your FrogLog yet.
- **Games in folders you've told it to watch**, under **Configure... > Non-Steam Games…** (Windows and Linux).
- **Games you've deleted on the website** that were still linked in LilyPad.

To turn this off, untick **Detect games not in your FrogLog library** in Configure, or **Record games not in FrogLog** on Steam Deck.

## Sorting out a new game

Open **New Games** from the tray menu (or the Steam Deck panel). Each game shows the hours and number of sessions recorded so far, with three choices.

On Steam Deck, **Create New** is called **Add to FrogLog**, and **Create and log to new entry (replay)** is called **New entry (replay)**. They work the same way.

> **[Screenshot]** The New Games window with one game, showing Create New, Map to Existing and Dismiss.

### Create New

Adds the game to your FrogLog, with all its recorded sessions.

LilyPad looks the game up and suggests a match. Click **Create** to accept it, or **Search Manually Instead** to search yourself. You can search by title, or by Steam app id, for example `appid:12345`. Pick the right result and click **Use Selected**.

### Map to Existing

Logs the time against a game you already have, for example one listed under a slightly different name. Choose the game from the list and click **Log Hours**.

If the game you pick is already Completed or DNF, logging hours to it removes its end date and puts it back to In Progress.

### Dismiss

Throws the recorded time away.

## Games you've already finished

If the game is in your FrogLog but already marked Completed or DNF, LilyPad asks which you mean:

- **Log to most recent entry** adds the time to your finished playthrough. This removes its end date and puts it back to In Progress.
- **Create and log to new entry (replay)** starts a new playthrough, linked to the earlier one as a [replay](/library/replays-and-dnf).
- **Dismiss** throws the time away.
