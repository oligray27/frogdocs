# Year in Review

Your Year in Review is one page about your year on FrogLog: what you played, how much, when, and how you compare with everyone else.

## When it's ready

Each year's Review comes out on **1 January** of the following year, so your 2026 Year in Review opens on 1 January 2027. Reviews for 2024 and 2025 open on the same day. FrogLog didn't log sessions back then, so those are built from your games' total hours, completions and trophies. You get a notification when it's ready, and a **Year in Review** menu appears on your [Stats](/stats) page, listing the years you can open.

Before then the page shows how many days are left. Everything you log until the end of the year counts.

## What's in it

> **[Screenshot]** The top of a Year in Review: the year, Highlights and Trophies.

- **Highlights**: how many games you played (live service included), how that compares with last year, how many were new to you, and your hours and sessions.
- **Trophies**: the trophies you unlocked that year on Steam, PlayStation and Xbox (Steam and Xbox call them achievements), and across how many games. You only see this if you've [linked one of those accounts](/platforms).
- **How you tracked**: how many of your sessions came from [LilyPad](/lilypad), [Quick Session](/library/sessions), each [linked platform's](/platforms) automatic tracking, or somewhere else.
- **Your top games**: your three most played games by hours, with each one's share of your play time and how many sessions you played.
- **How you compare**: your trophies, games played and longest streak, each in its own card. The bar fills to the share of other players you're ahead of, so it's only full if you hold the record. The arrow in the middle is the FrogLog median, labelled with its value: past it, you did better than most. Who you're compared with is explained below.
- **New, recent and classic**: how your play time splits between games that came out that year, in the five years before, or earlier, with the biggest share called out.
- **By the numbers**: days played, games completed, new games, your average session, your busiest month and how many platforms you played on.
- **What you played**: a chart of the Steam tags you spent the most time in, such as RPG or Open World. Switch it to **Genres** to see your genres instead. Games without Steam tags aren't counted in the Tags view.

> **[Screenshot]** One of the top five game panels, with its monthly chart and trophies.

- **Your top games, up close**: your top five, each over its own art. Each one shows its share of your play time, your sessions, your longest streak in it, a chart of which months you played it, and the trophies you unlocked in it that year. Hover over (or tap) a trophy to see its name, what it's for and when you unlocked it; **View All Trophies** opens the game's full trophy list. Other people see a hidden trophy's icon and date, but not its name or description. **Game Page** and **View in Library** take you to the game.
- **Where you played**: the platform you played most on, with your games, sessions and hours there and its five most played games, then every platform's share of your play time. Each game counts on the platform it's logged on.
- **Your play time by month**: each month's share of your year, with your top five picked out in their own colours. Hover over (or tap) a month to see how it split between them.
- **Your longest streak**: your longest run of days in a row with at least one session, as a line with a mark for each day (hover over a day to see what you played), and the games you played during it.
- **Every game you played**: every game you played that year, month by month. **New** marks the ones you started that year, and the other badge is the game's share of that month's play time; **Show more** lists the rest of a busy month. Switch to **Grid** to see each game once, with its share of the whole year. Click a cover to open the game.

At the end, **Questions** answers the common ones about where the numbers come from.

## How it's counted

- **Hours** come from sessions with a date. A game without session tracking only has a total, so all of it counts in the year you started the game, spread evenly from its start date to its end date (or to the end of the year if it carried on). A game you started the year before still counts as played, but its hours stay with the year you started it. Untracked games don't add sessions, days played or streaks. Time added with [Sync Playtime](/library/editing-games) (or caught up automatically from Nintendo Switch) is spread evenly from when you last played the game to the day it was synced, so it lands in the months it was most likely played; it doesn't add sessions or days either. Pre-tracked hours and sessions over 24 hours are left out.
- **A new game** is one you hadn't played before that year: no sessions and no start date before 1 January. A replay of something you'd played before isn't new, and nor is a game with undated play (pre-tracked hours or synced playtime), since that could be from any time.
- **How you tracked** has been recorded for every session since November 2026. Older sessions are sorted by their notes, so the 2026 split is an estimate.
- **Trophies** are refreshed for everyone at the end of the year. Nintendo Switch has no trophies to count.

The Review is worked out from your library each time you open it. If you add or fix sessions after 1 January, it updates straight away.

## Who you're compared with

**How you compare** compares you with everyone who logged at least one session that year. For trophies, it only counts people with a linked Steam, PlayStation or Xbox account. You only see the median and where you sit. Nobody's name or numbers are shown.

## Sharing it

Only you can see your Year in Review until you share it. At the top of the page, set **Who can see your Year in Review** to **Public**, then use **Share** to copy its link or send it.

> **[Screenshot]** The Who can see your Year in Review selector set to Public, with the Share button.

Once it's shared:

- Anyone who can see your profile can open it, from the **Year in Review** menu on your **Stats** tab or from the link. Your [privacy settings](/social/privacy) still apply: people on your **Invisible To** list can't see it, and people without an account can only see it if your profile is visible outside FrogLog.
- **Private games still count** in your totals, top-game shares and charts, but other people don't see their names, covers or trophies. Your top five can be shorter for them, and private games show as **Private games** in your streak.

Set it back to **Private** to stop sharing at any time. Each year is shared separately.

To see someone else's, open their FrogLog and go to their **Stats** tab. If they've shared a Year in Review, there's a **Year in Review** menu beside **Compare**, listing the years they've shared.
