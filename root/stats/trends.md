# Trends

**Trends** draws your playtime as a chart, so you can see how it changes over time, compare yourself with friends, or see who's played a game the most.

Trends is only available on desktop.

> **[Screenshot]** The Trends page with a few pens plotted on a time chart.

## Setting up a chart

Click **Chart Settings** to open the settings drawer. Make your changes, then click **Apply**.

### Query By

- **User**: each line on the chart is one person and one statistic, for example "my hours on PlayStation" against "a friend's hours on PlayStation".
- **Game**: pick one **Game**, and each line is a person's playtime on that game.

### Chart Type

- **Time**: a line chart over a period of time.
- **Bar**: one bar per line, showing all-time **Total Hours**.

### Pens

Each line on the chart is a **pen**. You can set up to five.

For each pen, choose:

1. A **user**: yourself or anyone you follow.
2. A **statistic**:
   - **Hours Played**: hours per day
   - **Sessions Count**: sessions per day
   - **Cumulative Hours**: a running total
   - **Hours in Genre**: hours per day in one genre you pick
   - **Hours on Platform**: hours per day on one platform you pick

   When querying by Game, the choices are **Hours Played**, **Sessions Count** and **Cumulative Hours** for that game.

Platforms marked with an asterisk, such as **PlayStation\* (any)**, combine every version of that platform, for example PS4 and PS5 together.

Use **Apply this stat to all pens** to copy the first pen's statistic to the others, and **Clear pen** to empty one.

### Y-Axis

- **Individual per pen**: each line has its own scale. This is best when the numbers are very different sizes.
- **Same for all pens**: one shared scale. This is best for a fair side-by-side comparison.

### Time Period

**Last 7 Days**, **Last 14 Days**, **Last 30 Days**, **Last 3 Months**, **Last 6 Months**, **Last Year**, or **Custom** with your own **From** and **To** dates.

## What's included

- **Time** charts only use games with [session tracking](/library/sessions), because FrogLog needs to know which day each hour was played. Your [Stats](/stats) page covers games without session tracking.
- **Bar** charts use all-time Total Hours, including games without session tracking.
- Single sessions over 24 hours are left out of Time charts, as on the Stats page.
- Other people's private games and sessions are never included.
