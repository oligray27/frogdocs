# Comparing Stats

You can put your stats side by side with anyone you follow.

## Starting a comparison

On your **Stats** page, open **Compare against** and pick someone. Only people you follow are listed. Follow people from the [Social](/social) page first.

You can also open someone's FrogLog and go to their **Stats** tab, which shows their stats compared with yours.

Pick **No comparison** to go back to just your own.

> **[Screenshot]** The Stats page comparing two users in Overlay mode.

## Overlay or Split

Once you're comparing, two buttons choose the layout:

- **Overlay** puts both people's numbers on the same cards and charts. Each card shows both values, with the higher one highlighted. Charts draw both people's data together, in different colours.
- **Split** shows two complete sets of stats side by side, one for each person.

Everything else works as normal: the Games, Live Service and Combined tabs, the year menu, and the top lists all apply to both people at once.

## What's included

You only see what the other person has made public. Their private games and private sessions aren't counted in what you see. See [Privacy](/social/privacy).

The same goes the other way: when someone compares against you, they only see your public games and sessions. So if you keep some things private, your numbers can look lower to them than they do to you.
