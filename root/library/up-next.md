# Up Next

**Up Next** is your wishlist: games you want to play but haven't started yet.

## Adding games

On the Search page, click **Add to Up Next** on any result. It's added straight away, with no form to fill in. Games coming out soon work too: the details card counts down the days until release.

![The Up Next page with a few games and a details card](/assets/fl-library-up-next/upnext.webp)

## Ordering your list

Up Next keeps whatever order you put it in, so you can use it as a play queue.

1. Click **Reorder** in the toolbar.
2. Drag games (rows in table view, covers in grid view) into the order you want.

Your order is the default sort, shown as **Custom order** in the toolbar's sort menu. You can also sort by **Title**, **Release date** or **Date added**, and the arrow button next to the menu flips the direction. Clicking **Reorder** switches back to **Custom order**.

## Starting a game

When you start playing something on your list:

1. Select it and click **Add to Games** on its details card.
2. The **Move from Up Next to Games** form opens, with the game's details filled in. Add the platform, start date and anything else, then click **Move to Games**.

The game leaves Up Next and appears in your library. If you've played it before, it's offered as a [replay](/library/replays-and-dnf).

To remove a game without moving it, click **Edit**, then **Delete**.

## Syncing your Steam wishlist

If you've linked Steam, a **Sync Wishlist from Steam** button appears in the Up Next toolbar.

This is a one-way sync. Your **entire** Up Next list is deleted and replaced with what's currently on your Steam wishlist, including any games you added by hand. It can't be undone.

1. Click **Sync Wishlist from Steam**.
2. Read the warning, then click **Sync & Replace**, then **Confirm? Can't be undone**.
3. Wait while FrogLog looks up each game. It then shows how many games were synced.

Your Steam wishlist must be public for this to work.
