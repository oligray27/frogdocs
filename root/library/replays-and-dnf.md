# Replays and DNF

## Replays

Each time you play a game through, it gets its own entry, with its own dates, hours, rating and review. A second (or third) playthrough is a **replay**.

### Logging a replay

Add the game again from Search as usual. If it's already in your library, the **Replay** box in the Add New Game form is ticked for you. Fill in the new playthrough's details and save.

- If the earlier playthrough is finished, the new entry is linked to it as a replay.
- If you haven't finished the earlier one, FrogLog asks first. **Attach To Existing Entry** keeps playing that one (it just adds the new platform to it), and **Log Separately** starts a new playthrough linked as a replay. See [Adding Games](/library/adding-games).

You can also tick **Replay** for a game that isn't in your library yet, for example one you played before you joined FrogLog. It's marked as a replay with nothing to link to.

Untick **Replay** if FrogLog guessed wrong, such as a remake that shares its name with the original.

### How replays look

Replays show a circular arrow icon in your library. The details card shows each playthrough's own hours, plus a total across every playthrough.

By default each playthrough is its own row. To tuck replays under the original instead, set **Profile > Settings > Library Defaults > Group Replays in Table** to **Grouped**. Click the arrow next to a game's title to expand or collapse its replays.

## DNF

**DNF** (did not finish) is for games you stopped playing and don't plan to finish. Tick **DNF** in the add or edit form.

- The game's status shows as **DNF**, in red.
- The **End Date** field is blanked out while DNF is ticked, and DNF takes priority over any end date.
- DNF can't be combined with a **Game Status (Override)** of Completed or Dormant.

If you're only taking a break, use **Dormant** instead. See [Editing Games](/library/editing-games).

Later decide to finish it after all? Untick **DNF** and add an end date. Or, if you're starting over, log a replay.
