# Your Library

Your library is every game you've logged in FrogLog. You'll find it on the **Games** page, which is also where FrogLog opens when you sign in.

This section covers:

- [Adding Games](/library/adding-games): finding a game and adding it
- [Editing Games](/library/editing-games): every field on a game, statuses, ratings and reviews
- [Replays and DNF](/library/replays-and-dnf): playing a game again, and games you didn't finish
- [Sessions](/library/sessions): logging individual play sessions, and Quick Session
- [Live Service Games](/library/live-service-games): ongoing games with no real ending
- [Up Next](/library/up-next): your wishlist
- [Lists](/library/lists): collections of games
- [Screenshots](/library/screenshots): uploading and sharing screenshots
- [Trophies](/library/trophies): achievements and trophies from Steam, PlayStation and Xbox

## The Games page

The Games page has two parts: your games, shown as a table or a grid of covers, and a **details card** for whichever game you've selected.

> **[Screenshot]** The Games page on desktop, with the table on one side and a game's details card on the other.

On a phone, tapping a game opens its details on their own screen. Use the back button to return to your library.

### Games and Live Service tabs

The **Games** and **Live Service** tabs at the top switch between your regular games and your [live service games](/library/live-service-games). If you'd rather have one combined library, turn on **Unify Games & Live Service** in **Profile > Settings > Library Defaults**.

### Table or grid

Use the two layout buttons in the toolbar to switch between them:

- **Table view** shows one row per game, with columns for hours, rating, status and dates.
- **Grid view** shows cover art only. A slider sets how many covers fit on each row, and a dropdown sets the sort order.

You can choose which layout the Games page opens in under **Profile > Settings > Library Defaults > Default Table Layout**.

### Sorting

In table view, click a column heading to sort by it, and click it again to reverse the order. In grid view, use the sort dropdown and the arrow button next to it.

You can sort by title, hours played, rating, status, last played, release date, start date or end date. Live service games can also be sorted by number of sessions.

Your default sort for each table is set in **Profile > Settings > Library Defaults**.

### Searching and filtering

Type in the **Search games...** box to narrow the list by title.

**Filter** opens a panel where you can combine any of these:

- **Status**: Completed, In Progress, Dormant, DNF or Not Started
- **Rating**: one to five stars
- **Release Year** and **Hours Played**: drag the slider to set a range
- **Platform**, **Genre**, **Developer** and **Country**: type to pick one or more

**Clear All** resets every filter. If nothing matches, the table says so and offers a **Clear filters** button.

> **[Screenshot]** The Filters panel with a few filters selected.

### Row icons

Small icons next to a game's title show extra information at a glance:

| Icon | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Circular arrow | A [replay](/library/replays-and-dnf) |
| Padlock | Private: hidden from other users |
| Eye | Its sessions are visible to other users |
| Notebook | [Session tracking](/library/sessions) is on |
| Star | You've written a review |
| Picture | It has [screenshots](/library/screenshots) |
| Fork | A mod or fork of another game |

On a phone, a coloured dot at the end of each row shows the game's status. You can hide it with **Profile > Settings > Library Defaults > Mobile Status Dot**.

### Keyboard

On desktop, the up and down arrow keys move through the table.

## The details card

Selecting a game shows its details card, with:

- The game's artwork. Click the cover to expand or shrink it. **Profile > Settings > Display & Layout > Default Details Card Image Size** sets which size it starts at.
- When the artwork is expanded, the game's logo in place of its name, if it has one. Turn this off with **Profile > Settings > Display & Layout > Show Steam Title Image**.
- Badges for your rating, platform, hours, genre, developer and release date.
- A date badge showing when you started and finished, or how long you've been playing. Click it to switch between the start date and "started N days ago".
- **Description** and **Your Review** tabs.
- **Trophies** and **Screenshots** buttons, when the game has them.
- **Edit**, which opens the [edit form](/library/editing-games).
- **Add Session**, **Quick Session** and **View Sessions** for games that use [sessions](/library/sessions).
- A **Steam** or **IGDB** link to the game's store or database page.

## Moving games between Games and Live Service

To move one game to Live Service, open its edit form and click **Move to Live Service**.

To move several games at once, or to move a live service game back to Games:

1. Click **Transfer** in the toolbar.
2. Tick the games you want to move.
3. Click **Move to Live Service** (or **Move to Games** on the Live Service tab).
