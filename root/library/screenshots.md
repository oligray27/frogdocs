# Screenshots

You can attach up to 10 screenshots to each game in your library.

## Uploading

1. Select the game and click **Screenshots** on its details card. The number next to it is how many it has.
2. Click **Add Photo** and choose an image. JPG, PNG, GIF and WebP files up to 10 MB are accepted.
3. In **Tag Screenshot**, tick **Contains spoilers** or **NSFW** if they apply, and add an optional **Caption**.
4. Click **Upload**.

![The Screenshots window for a game, with the Add Photo tile](/assets/fl-library-screenshots/screenshotwindow.png)

## Viewing

Click a screenshot to open it full size. Its caption appears underneath.

Screenshots tagged **Contains spoilers** or **NSFW** are blurred, with a label saying which. Tap or click to reveal them.

## Managing your screenshots

Each of your screenshots has three buttons:

- **Edit tags/caption**: change the spoiler and NSFW tags or the caption.
- **Pin to showcase**: show it in the Screenshot Showcase on your profile. You can pin up to 10 screenshots in total, across all your games. Manage and reorder pinned screenshots in **Profile > Settings > Showcases**.
- **Delete screenshot**.

## Who can see them

Other users can see the screenshots on any of your public games, from the game's details card on your profile or from your profile's **Screenshots** tab (see [Social](/social)), which gathers every screenshot you've uploaded, grouped by game. Screenshots on [private](/library/editing-games) games stay hidden.

Other users can [like](/social/likes) your screenshots.
