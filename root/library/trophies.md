# Trophies

FrogLog shows a game's achievements or trophies from Steam, PlayStation and Xbox, all under the name **trophies**.

## Seeing a game's trophies

When a game is linked to Steam, PlayStation or Xbox and has trophies, its details card shows a **Trophies** button. The number beside it is how many you've unlocked out of the total, or just the total if your account isn't linked.

Click it to see every trophy, with its icon, description and the date you unlocked it. Locked trophies are faded with a padlock.

If a game has trophies on more than one platform, buttons at the top switch between them.

> **[Screenshot]** The Trophies window for a game, with some unlocked and some locked trophies.

### Hidden trophies

Trophies the developer has marked as hidden show as **Hidden Trophy** until you unlock them. Click one to reveal its name and description if you don't mind spoilers.

## Tracking your progress

To see which trophies you've unlocked, link the platform to your FrogLog account from your **Profile**:

- **Steam**: link your Steam account. Your Steam profile's game details must be public.
- **PlayStation**: link your PSN account.
- **Xbox**: link your Xbox account.

Until a platform is linked, the Trophies window shows every trophy as locked, with a reminder to link it.

## Pinning trophies to your profile

Your profile has a **Trophy Cabinet** showing up to 12 trophies you're proud of.

Click the pin button on any unlocked trophy to add it (**Pin to profile**), and click it again to remove it (**Unpin**). Once the cabinet is full, unpin one first. You can reorder your pinned trophies in **Profile > Settings > Showcases**.

## Hiding trophies

If a game's trophies aren't relevant to you, tick **No Trophies** in its edit form. The Trophies button disappears from that game.
