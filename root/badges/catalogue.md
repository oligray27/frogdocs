# Badge Catalogue

Every badge you can earn, and what it takes. See [Badges](/badges) for how badges work, including what counts and what doesn't.

For ladders, the numbers are what you need to reach each tier. For example, **Library** Gold needs 250 games logged.

## Ladders

| Badge | Counts | Bronze | Silver | Gold | Diamond | Platinum |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Service** | How long you've been a member of FrogLog. | 1 year | 2 years | 3 years | 4 years | 5 years |
| **Library** | Games you've added to your log. | 50 | 100 | 250 | 500 | 1,000 |
| **Completionist** | Games you've seen through to the end. | 25 | 75 | 150 | 300 | 600 |
| **Sessions** | Play sessions you've logged, across games and live service. | 100 | 300 | 750 | 1,500 | 3,000 |
| **Hours** | Total time you've logged across everything you play. | 500 | 2,500 | 7,500 | 15,000 | 30,000 |
| **Consistency** | Different days you've logged a session on. They don't need to be in a row. | 30 | 100 | 250 | 365 | 730 |
| **Streak** | Your longest run of consecutive days with a session. | 10 | 30 | 60 | 100 | 200 |
| **Critic** | Games you've given a rating. | 25 | 75 | 150 | 300 | 600 |
| **Reviewer** | Games you've written a review for. | 5 | 10 | 25 | 50 | 100 |
| **Photographer** | Screenshots you've uploaded. | 20 | 50 | 100 | 250 | 500 |
| **Curator** | Lists you've made with at least 5 games in them. | 3 | 5 | 10 | 20 | 40 |
| **Genre Traveller** | Different genres across your log. | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 | 30 |
| **Globetrotter** | Different countries the studios behind your games come from. | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
| **Devotee** | The most games you've logged from a single developer. | 5 | 10 | 20 | 30 | 50 |
| **No Shame** | Games you've DNF'd. Knowing when to quit is a skill. | 5 | 10 | 25 | 50 | 100 |
| **Replayer** | Games you've come back to for another playthrough. | 3 | 5 | 10 | 20 | 40 |

## Genres

| Badge | Counts | Bronze | Silver | Gold | Diamond | Platinum |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **It's Dangerous to Go Alone** | Adventure games you've logged. | 15 | 40 | 80 | 150 | 300 |
| **Rip and Tear** | Shooter games you've logged. | 10 | 25 | 50 | 100 | 200 |
| **You're Finally Awake** | Role-playing games you've logged. | 10 | 25 | 50 | 100 | 200 |
| **Jimbo Says Hi** | Indie games you've logged. | 5 | 15 | 30 | 60 | 120 |
| **It's-a Me** | Platform games you've logged. | 5 | 15 | 30 | 60 | 120 |
| **The Factory Must Grow** | Simulator games you've logged. | 5 | 15 | 30 | 60 | 120 |
| **The Cake Is a Lie** | Puzzle games you've logged. | 5 | 15 | 30 | 60 | 120 |
| **Jackpot!** | Hack and slash games you've logged. | 5 | 15 | 30 | 60 | 120 |
| **Armchair General** | Strategy and tactics games you've logged. | 5 | 15 | 30 | 60 | 120 |
| **Blue Shelled** | Racing games you've logged. | 3 | 10 | 20 | 40 | 80 |
| **The Big Leagues** | Sport games you've logged. | 3 | 10 | 20 | 40 | 80 |
| **Get Over Here!** | Fighting games you've logged. | 3 | 10 | 20 | 40 | 80 |

A game counts towards a genre ladder if that genre is one of its genres, so one game can count towards several. **Armchair General** includes real-time strategy, turn-based strategy and tactical games. There's no ladder for Action, as it isn't used consistently across games.

## Moments

| Badge | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| **Speed Runner** | Complete a game in under 5 hours. |
| **Full Cycle** | Start and finish a game in the same month. |
| **Tough Crowd** | Rate a game 1 star or less. |
| **Masterpiece** | Rate a game 5 stars. |
| **Full Spectrum** | Use every star rating from 1 to 5. |
| **Time Traveller** | Log games from 5 different decades. |

## Secrets

There are also a few secret badges, hidden around FrogLog for you to find. They stay hidden, even on your own Badges page, until you earn them, and the page shows how many are still out there.
