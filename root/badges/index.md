# Badges

Badges reward you for how you use FrogLog: games logged and finished, sessions played, reviews written, screenshots shared, and more. Open **Badges** from the navigation to see what you've earned and how close you are to the next one.

The [Badge Catalogue](/badges/catalogue) lists every badge and what it takes.

![The Badges page, showing the summary, leaderboard and a few ladder cards](/assets/fl-badges/badgespage.png)

## Two kinds of badge

### Ladders

Most badges are **ladders** with five tiers: **Bronze**, **Silver**, **Gold**, **Diamond** and **Platinum**. You earn them in order, and each tier needs more than the last. For example, the Library ladder starts at 50 games logged and tops out at 1,000.

Each ladder's card shows your current tier and a progress bar towards the next one. Genre ladders, which count the games you've logged in a particular genre, have their own **Genres** section.

### One-offs

The rest are one-off badges, in four groups:

- **Moments**: one-time achievements, like finishing a game in under five hours.
- **Connections**: linking your platforms and using automatic tracking.
- **Commemorative**: being an early member, or holding the Weekly Playtime Crown.
- **Secrets**: a few hidden badges you'll have to find for yourself. They show as **Hidden Badge** until you earn them.

## What counts

- **Imported games don't count** until you've used them. A game still in the **Imported** status (see [Platforms & Automatic Tracking](/platforms)) adds nothing to your badges. Play it, or edit and save it, and it counts in full, including its history.
- **Live service games** count once they have at least one dated session.
- **All your hours count**, including big lumps of earlier playtime such as "Pre-tracked hours".
- **Sessions, Consistency and Streak** only count sessions with a date.
- **Connection badges** and **Service** need you to have logged at least one game or session.
- **Replays** don't count twice towards genre ladders.

Badges are checked whenever you change something, so new ones appear straight away. Service, which counts how long you've been a member, is checked daily.

When you earn a badge or a new tier, you get a [notification](/social/notifications).

## Pinning badges

Show off up to 5 badges on your profile card.

- On the Badges page, click the pin button on any badge you've earned (**Pin badge**). Click it again to unpin it.
- Reorder or unpin your badges in **Profile > Settings > Showcases**, under **Pinned Badges**. See [Showcases](/profile-settings/showcases).

## The leaderboard

The **Badge Leaderboard** on the Badges page ranks you against the people you follow, by points:

- Each ladder tier is worth its tier number: Bronze 1, Silver 2, Gold 3, Diamond 4 and Platinum 5. Every tier you pass through counts, so a ladder at Gold is worth 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 points.
- Each one-off badge is worth 1 point.

The summary at the top of the page shows how many badges you've earned across all tiers, and how many ladders you have at each tier.
