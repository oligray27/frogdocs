# Profile Card

Your **profile card** is what people see when they click on you, for example on the Social or Activity pages. A smaller version, the **mini profile card**, is what appears on the Social page. Customise both in **Profile > Settings > Profile Card**.

Use **Preview Profile Card** and **Preview Mini Profile Card** to see how they look to other people.

![A customised profile card, with a banner, profile colours and an avatar decoration](/assets/fl-social/customprofilecard.png)

## Banner

The banner is the image across the top of your card. Click **Upload Banner** (or **Change Banner**), choose or drag in an image (JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP, up to 10 MB), drag to choose which part to show, and click **Save**. **Remove Banner** takes it off.

## Profile Colours

Colour your card to match your style. Under **Primary**, pick one of the preset swatches. Clear it with the **X** to go back to the default.

### Colours from your banner

Tick **Generate colours from banner image** to have FrogLog pick colours to match your banner. A swatch made from your banner appears among the presets, and it updates when you upload a new banner.

- **Generated Theme Style**: **Solid** for a single colour, or **Gradient** for a blend.
- **Animate gradient**: makes a gradient slowly shift. Only available for gradients.

## Avatar Decoration

Add a frame or effect around your profile photo. Open the **Avatar Decoration** menu, search or browse, and pick one. Choose **None** to remove it.
