# Profile

The **Profile** section of your settings covers who you are on FrogLog. Themes, backgrounds and theme music are also here, and are covered in [Appearance](/profile-settings/appearance).

## Photo

Click **Upload Photo** (or **Change Photo**), then choose or drag in an image. JPG, PNG, GIF and WebP files up to 5 MB are accepted. Drag to choose which part to show, then click **Save**. **Reset crop** starts again.

**Remove Photo** goes back to the default, which shows your initial.

You can also change your photo by clicking it on your profile card.

## Username

Click **Change Username**, enter a **New Username**, and save. Usernames must be unique.

If you use [LilyPad](/lilypad), log in to it with your new username.

## Password

Click **Change Password**, then enter your **Current Password**, a **New Password**, and the new password again under **Confirm New Password**.

## Bio

Write a few lines about yourself under **Bio**. It shows on your profile card.

## Favourite Games

Pick up to 4 favourite games to show on your profile card. Use **Reorder favourites** to change their order.

## Bottom Navigation (Mobile)

On a phone, the bar at the bottom of the screen has five buttons. Choose which pages they go to with **Assign slots…**. The options are Games, Up Next, Activity, Lists, Profile, Stats, Social, Add New, What's New, Notifs. and Badges.
