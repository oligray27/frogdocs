# Appearance

Make FrogLog look the way you like. These settings are in **Profile > Settings > Profile**.

## Themes

Choose a theme from **Appearance**. It changes the colours of the whole site.

| Theme | |
|---|---|
| **Light** | FrogLog's standard light look |
| **Dark** | FrogLog's standard dark look |
| **NieR:Automata** | Inspired by NieR:Automata |
| **Frogboxd** | Inspired by Letterboxd |
| **FrogLog 2077** | Inspired by Cyberpunk 2077 |
| **Catppuccin Latte** / **Catppuccin Mocha** | The Catppuccin palette, light and dark |
| **Rosé Pine Dawn** / **Rosé Pine** | The Rosé Pine palette, light and dark |
| **Gruvbox Light** / **Gruvbox** | The Gruvbox palette, light and dark |
| **Solarized** | The Solarized palette |
| **Nord** | The Nord palette |
| **Terminal** | Green on black |

> **[Screenshot]** A few themes side by side.

Your theme also shows when other people visit your profile, unless you turn off **Make Theme Public** in **Social Visibility**.

## Custom background

On desktop, you can put your own colour or image behind the whole site. Click **Change Background**:

- **Colour**: pick a colour, or type its **Hex colour**.
- **Image**: click **Choose an image** to upload a JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP. Then choose:
  - **Size**: **Cover** fills the screen, **Contain** shows the whole image, **Tile** repeats it.
  - **Position**: **Top**, **Center** or **Bottom**.
  - **Blur**: soften the image so the page stays easy to read.

Custom backgrounds aren't shown on phones, so the button is greyed out there.

Visitors to your profile see your background too, unless you turn off **Make Custom Background Public** in **Social Visibility**.

## Profile Theme Audio

Give your profile its own theme music, which plays when people visit your profile.

1. Under **Profile Theme Audio**, click **Upload**.
2. Give it a **Track Name**, for example "Song of Storms - Legend of Zelda".
3. Choose an audio file: MP3, M4A, OGG or WAV, up to 15 MB.
4. Trim it to the part you want to play, then save.

Once uploaded, you can play it, **Edit** the trim, or **Remove** it.

Visitors choose whether theme music plays automatically, with **Others' Theme Audio** in their own **Social Visibility** settings.

There are a few secrets hidden around FrogLog for you to find, and some of them change how it looks.
