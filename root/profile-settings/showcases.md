# Showcases

Your profile card can show off three collections. Manage all three in **Profile > Settings > Showcases**.

| Showcase | Holds | How to add to it |
|---|---|---|
| **Pinned Badges** | Up to 5 badges | Click **Pin badge** on a badge you've earned, on the [Badges](/badges) page |
| **Trophy Cabinet** | Up to 12 trophies | Click **Pin to profile** on an unlocked trophy in a game's Trophies window. See [Trophies](/library/trophies). |
| **Screenshot Showcase** | Up to 10 screenshots | Click **Pin to showcase** on a screenshot. See [Screenshots](/library/screenshots). |

In Showcases you can:

- Reorder each one with its reorder button (**Reorder badges**, **Reorder trophies**, **Reorder screenshots**), then drag things into place. Click it again when you're done.
- Remove anything with **Unpin**.

![The Showcases settings, showing pinned badges, the Trophy Cabinet and the Screenshot Showcase](/assets/fl-settings-showcases/showcasessettings.png)

Hidden trophies you've pinned show as **Hidden Achievement** to visitors until they click to reveal them.
