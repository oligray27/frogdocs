# Xbox

Linking your Xbox account lets FrogLog import your Xbox history, track your sessions automatically, and show your achievements. It covers both Xbox consoles and the Xbox app on PC.

## Linking

1. Open your **Profile** and click **Link Xbox**.
2. Sign in with your Microsoft account on Microsoft's own page, and allow FrogLog access.
3. You're sent back to FrogLog, and the button now shows your gamertag and avatar.

FrogLog never sees your Microsoft password.

If Microsoft ever stops accepting FrogLog's access, the button changes to **Relink Xbox**. Click it and sign in again.

## Importing

1. Click the **Import** button next to your gamertag.
2. Optionally, set **Only import games played for at least** a number of hours.
3. Optionally, tick **Do not import games that have only been played via Xbox for PC**, if you track your PC games another way.
4. Start the import, then choose what to do with any games you already have. See [Importing](/platforms).

## Automatic tracking

Turn on **Automatic Xbox Session Tracking** in **Profile > Settings > Autonomous Tracking > Xbox**. It covers console play and the Xbox app on PC. Games you play that aren't in your library are added automatically. See [How it works](/platforms).

If you also use LilyPad on your PC, don't use both for the same PC games, or sessions will be logged twice.

To show what you're playing without logging sessions, use **Mirror Xbox In-Game Presence to FrogLog** instead.

## Achievements

Games linked to Xbox show their achievements under **Trophies**, including which you've unlocked. See [Trophies](/library/trophies).

## Unlinking

Click the **Unlink** button next to your gamertag. Games and sessions already in your library are kept.
