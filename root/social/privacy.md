# Privacy

Your FrogLog is public by default, so other users can see your library, reviews, lists and what you're playing. You can keep any part of it to yourself.

## Private games and sessions

- **A game:** untick **Public** in its edit form. Other people no longer see it in your library or Activity. See [Editing Games](/library/editing-games).
- **A game's sessions:** a game's sessions are only visible to others if **Public Sessions** is ticked in its edit form.
- **A single session:** use the **Hide from public** button on it in the sessions list.
- **Sessions from automatic tracking:** set the default for each platform with **Public Sessions** in **Profile > Settings > Autonomous Tracking**.

## Private lists

New lists are public. To keep one to yourself, turn on editing on the Lists page and click its **Public** button to switch it to **Private**. See [Lists](/library/lists).

## Invisible To

To hide from particular people without making everything private, add them to your **Invisible To** list in **Profile > Settings > Social Visibility**.

1. Pick a user.
2. Choose how invisible you want to be:
   - **Online & Sessions**: they can still see your profile and library, but not whether you're online, what you're playing, when you were last seen, or any of your sessions.
   - **Whole Account**: to them, your account doesn't exist. They can't find your profile, and nothing of yours appears in their Activity, Highlights or Online Now.
3. Add them.

You can change someone's mode or **Remove** them from the list at any time.

People aren't told when you add them. If you're invisible to someone, following or liking their things doesn't send them a notification either, so you don't give yourself away.

## Your profile's look

These settings in **Profile > Settings > Social Visibility** control whether other people see your profile's customisations:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Make Theme Public** | Whether people visiting your profile see your theme |
| **Make Custom Background Public** | Whether people visiting your profile see your custom background |
| **Show Steam Links** | Whether your games show links to their Steam store pages |
