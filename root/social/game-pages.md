# Game Pages

Every game on FrogLog has its own page, shared by everyone. It shows the game's details, who on FrogLog has it, how long it takes, what people thought of it, and their screenshots and clips.

> **[Screenshot]** A game page on desktop: the cover, title and buttons at the top, then the On FrogLog stats.

## Opening a game page

- Click a game's cover on the **Search** page, or in **Games Like This** on another game's page.
- Click the gamepad button in a game's details, on your own games, someone else's, or your [Up Next](/library/up-next) list.
- On the [Activity](/social/activity) page, click a game in **New From Friends**, or a game's name in the feed.

You don't need to be logged in to see a game page. Visitors who aren't logged in only see people who have chosen to share their profile outside FrogLog (see [Privacy](/social/privacy)).

On a phone, use **Back** at the top of the page to return to where you came from. Coming back to Search shows the same results, scrolled to where you were.

## The top of the page

The game's cover, title, genres, developer and release date, with its Steam tags, **Metacritic** score and **HLTB** time when you're logged in.

If the game isn't in your Games yet:

- **Add to Games** opens the **Add New Game** form, the same as on Search (see [Adding Games](/library/adding-games)).
- **Add to Up Next** adds it to your [Up Next](/library/up-next) list.

If it is, a line says how you've played it, such as **You're currently playing this game** or **You've played this game twice**, with:

- **View in your FrogLog**, which opens it in your Games.
- **Log Replay**, which opens the **Add New Game** form with **Replay** already ticked (see [Replays and DNF](/library/replays-and-dnf)).

There's also a **Steam** or **IGDB** link, and a **Share** button. A shared link shows the game's art and description when you post it in Discord and elsewhere.

If you're not logged in, **Sign in to log, rate or review** takes you to the sign-in page and brings you back afterwards.

## On FrogLog

| Tile | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **In Libraries** | How many people have the game, including games imported and not started yet |
| **Hours Logged** | Everyone's hours added together, including playtime imported from Steam, PlayStation or Xbox |
| **Days Played** | Each person's days with a play session, added together. **N/A** when nobody tracks sessions for it, or their sessions aren't public |
| **FrogRank** | Where the game places among every game played on FrogLog. Click the **?** beside it to see how it's worked out |
| **Average Rating** | Shown once at least 3 people have rated it |
| **Avg Completion** | The average hours to finish, from first playthroughs only. Shown once at least 3 people have finished it with their hours logged |

### FrogRank

FrogRank scores each game from three things: how many people have played it, how long they've played (each extra hour counts a little less than the last, so lots of players beats one person's marathon), and how it's rated against the FrogLog average. Ratings only make a big difference once a game has several. Only play logged on FrogLog counts: imported playtime, and hours carried over from before a game was tracked, don't. It's the same for everyone and updates every hour.

## Players

Everyone who has the game in their FrogLog: you first, then the people you follow, then everyone else. Each shows their status, hours and rating. Click someone to see their entry for the game.

## Reviews, Screenshots and Clips

**Reviews** lists everyone's written reviews, newest first. Long ones are shortened: click **Show more** to read the rest.

**Screenshots** and **Clips** show everyone's, with who uploaded each in the corner. Click one to view it. Screenshots and clips marked as a spoiler or NSFW aren't shown on game pages.

Use the flag on a review, screenshot or clip to report it to FrogLog's admins.

## Games Like This

Games similar to this one, with the same covers as Search: click a cover to open that game's page, or use **+** and the **Up Next** button on it.

## Hiding sections

Click a section's title to close or open it. FrogLog remembers which sections you've closed on that device, for every game page.

Only public games count anywhere on a game page, and everyone's [privacy](/social/privacy) settings apply, so people may see slightly different numbers.
