# Notifications

FrogLog lets you know when something happens that involves you.

## What you're notified about

| Notification | When |
|---|---|
| **[Name] followed you** | Someone starts following you |
| **[Name] liked your …** | Someone likes one of your games, sessions, reviews, screenshots or lists. Click the link to go straight to it. |
| **You earned the Weekly Playtime Crown!** | You've played the most hours of anyone on FrogLog in the last 7 days |
| **You lost the Weekly Playtime Crown** | Someone else has overtaken you |
| **You earned the … badge!** | You've earned a new badge, or a higher tier of one |
| **New update: …** | FrogLog has a new release. Click to read what's changed. |

## Reading notifications

On desktop, click the **bell** in the navigation bar. A red badge on it shows how many notifications you haven't read.

![The notifications dropdown open from the bell](/assets/fl-social-notifications/notificationstray.png)

- Click a notification to mark it as read, and to go to whatever it's about.
- **Delete notification** removes it.
- **Show more** loads older notifications.

On a phone, open **Inbox** from the menu. You can also put **Notifs.** in your bottom bar, with **Bottom Navigation (Mobile)** in **Profile > Settings > Profile**. It shows a red dot when you have something unread.

When there's nothing new, the Inbox says **You're All Caught Up**.
