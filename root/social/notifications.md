# Notifications

FrogLog lets you know when something happens that involves you.

## What you're notified about

| Notification | When |
|---|---|
| **[Name] followed you** | Someone starts following you |
| **[Name] liked your …** | Someone likes one of your games, sessions, reviews, screenshots or lists. Click the link to go straight to it. |
| **You earned the Weekly Playtime Crown!** | You've played the most hours of anyone on FrogLog in the last 7 days |
| **You lost the Weekly Playtime Crown** | Someone else has overtaken you |
| **You earned the … badge!** | You've earned a new badge, or a higher tier of one |
| **New update: …** | FrogLog has a new release. Click to read what's changed. |

## Reading notifications

On desktop, click the **bell** in the navigation bar. A red badge on it shows how many notifications you haven't read.

![The notifications dropdown open from the bell](/assets/fl-social-notifications/notificationstray.webp){width=30%}

- Click a notification to mark it as read, and to go to whatever it's about.
- **Delete notification** removes it.
- **Show more** loads older notifications.

On a phone, open **Inbox** from the menu. You can also put **Notifs.** in your bottom bar, with **Bottom Navigation (Mobile)** in **Profile > Settings > Profile**. It shows a red dot when you have something unread.

When there's nothing new, the Inbox says **You're All Caught Up**.

## Push notifications

FrogLog can also send notifications to your phone or computer, even when FrogLog isn't open. Set them up in **Profile > Settings > Notifications**.

1. Under **This Device**, choose **On**.
2. When your browser asks, choose **Allow**.
3. Click **Send Test Notification** to check it works.

Push notifications are turned on separately for each device, so repeat this on every phone or computer you want them on. Click or tap a notification to go straight to whatever it's about.

> **[Screenshot]** Profile > Settings > Notifications, with This Device set to On and a couple of devices listed.

### On iPhone and iPad

Safari only sends notifications to FrogLog once it's on your Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or later):

1. Open FrogLog in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open FrogLog from the new icon, and turn on notifications from there.

### Choosing what you're notified about

Untick anything you don't want under **Notify Me About**. This applies to all your devices, and doesn't change what shows in your Inbox.

| Option | When |
|---|---|
| **Follows** | Someone follows you |
| **Likes** | Someone likes one of your sessions, reviews, screenshots, lists or games |
| **Weekly Crown Earned** | You take the Weekly Playtime Crown |
| **Weekly Crown Lost** | Someone takes the Weekly Playtime Crown from you |
| **Badges Earned** | You earn a new badge or reach a new tier |
| **New Updates** | A new FrogLog update is posted |
| **Sessions Logged** | A session is logged for you automatically, by [LilyPad](/lilypad) or a [linked platform](/platforms) |
| **Friends Playing** | Someone you follow starts playing a game |

**Sessions Logged** and **Friends Playing** only come as push notifications. They don't appear in your Inbox.

### Your devices

**Your Devices** lists every device you've turned push notifications on for, with when each one last received one. Click **Remove**, then **Confirm?**, to stop sending to a device, for example a phone you no longer have.

If you blocked notifications for FrogLog earlier, your browser won't ask again. Allow them in your browser's site settings, then come back and choose **On**.
