# Likes

Show some love for other people's gaming by liking it. Look for the heart button on:

- **Games** in someone's library, on the details card
- **Reviews**, on the **Review** tab of a game's details card
- **Sessions**, in a game's sessions list and in the Activity feed
- **Screenshots**
- **Lists**

Click the heart to like something, and click it again to take your like back.

![A details card with the heart button, and the "Liked by" list showing](/assets/fl-social-likes/detailscardshowinglikes.png)

## Likes on your own things

You can't like your own games, reviews, sessions, screenshots or lists. Instead, the heart shows how many likes they have. Hover over it (or tap it) to see **Liked by**, with the names of who liked it.

Your profile card shows how many likes you've received in total.

When someone likes something of yours, you get a [notification](/social/notifications) that links straight to it.

## Who can like what

People can only like what they can see. Private games, private sessions and private lists can't be liked by anyone else. See [Privacy](/social/privacy).
