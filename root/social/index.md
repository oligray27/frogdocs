# Social

FrogLog is more fun with friends. Follow people to see what they're playing, browse their libraries and lists, like their sessions and screenshots, and compare stats.

This section covers:

- [Activity](/social/activity): what the people you follow have been up to
- [Likes](/social/likes): liking games, sessions, reviews, screenshots and lists
- [Notifications](/social/notifications): the bell and your Inbox
- [Privacy](/social/privacy): what other people can see, and hiding from specific users

## The Social page

Open **Social** from the navigation. It has two tabs:

- **All Users**: everyone on FrogLog.
- **Following**: just the people you follow.

Use **Search users...** to find someone by name.

![The Social page, showing a few user cards](/assets/fl-social/socialpage.webp)

### User cards

Each person has a card showing:

- Their avatar, name and banner
- What they're playing right now (**In-Game**), or their latest game
- How many games they've logged, and their total hours
- Their hours in the last 7 days. A gold crown means they've played the most of anyone on FrogLog this week: the **Weekly Playtime Crown**.
- When they joined, and when they were last seen

At the bottom of each card:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **View Profile** | Opens their FrogLog |
| **Follow** / **Unfollow** | Follow or stop following them |
| **Set nickname** | Give them a name that only you see, everywhere in FrogLog. Clear it to go back to their username. |
| **Hide user** | Remove them from your Social page. Unhide them from **Manage hidden users** in the toolbar. |

Click a card to see their **profile card**, with their bio, stats, favourite games, Trophy Cabinet, Screenshot Showcase, and follower and like counts.

![A user's profile card opened from the Social page](/assets/fl-social/customprofilecard.webp)

### Following

Following someone:

- Adds their activity to your [Activity](/social/activity) feed and Highlights
- Shows them in Online Now when they're playing
- Lets you compare your [lists](/library/lists) with theirs
- Sends them a [notification](/social/notifications)

On the **Following** tab, click **Reorder followed users** to drag the people you follow into the order you like.

## Viewing someone's FrogLog

**View Profile** opens their FrogLog. A bar under the navigation switches between their:

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Games** | Their library, with details cards just like yours. You can read their reviews, see their public sessions, and like things. |
| **Stats** | Their stats, compared with yours |
| **Lists** | Their public lists |
| **Screenshots** | Every screenshot they've uploaded, grouped by game |
| **Trophies** | Their trophies |
| **Badges** | The badges they've earned |

Private games and private sessions never appear. See [Privacy](/social/privacy).

### Their theme and background

People can give their profile its own theme, background and theme music. When you visit their FrogLog, you see their choices instead of yours, unless you've turned that off. (To set your own, see [Appearance](/profile-settings/appearance).) The settings are in **Profile > Settings > Social Visibility**:

- **View Others' Themes**
- **View Others' Custom Backgrounds**
- **Others' Theme Audio**: whether their music plays automatically (**Auto Play**) or waits for you (**Paused**)
