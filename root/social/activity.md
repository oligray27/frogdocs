# Activity

The **Activity** page shows what you and the people you follow have been playing. It has three parts: Online Now, the Feed, and Highlights.

If you don't follow anyone yet, you'll only see your own activity. Follow people from the [Social](/social) page to fill it up.

> **[Screenshot]** The Activity page on desktop, with Online Now, the feed and Highlights.

## Online Now

**Online Now** lists the people you follow, with who's playing what right now and when the others were last seen. Click someone to see their profile card.

People show as playing when they're running a Quick Session, using LilyPad, or have automatic tracking or presence mirroring turned on.

On a phone, a scrolling strip under the navigation bar shows who's playing right now.

## Feed

The **Feed** lists recent activity, newest first, with who did it and when:

- **Started a game**
- **Finished a game**
- **Gave up on a game** (DNF)
- **Wrote a review**
- **Logged a session**
- **Added to wishlist** (Up Next)
- **Created a list**

Use the two filters at the top to narrow it to particular people (**All users**) or particular kinds of activity (**All activity types**). The clear button resets both.

You can [like](/social/likes) sessions straight from the feed.

Your own activity is included. To see only other people's, set **Profile > Settings > Social Visibility > Show My Own Activity** to **Hide**.

## Highlights

**Highlights** gathers leaderboards and totals across you and the people you follow. Click a person's name in a tile to see their profile card.

> **[Screenshot]** The Highlights tab.

### Last 7 Days

| Tile | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Most Active** | Who played the most hours |
| **Longest Session** | The single longest session |
| **Total Hours** | Everyone's hours added together |
| **Sessions Logged** | How many sessions were logged |
| **Most Sessions** | Who logged the most sessions |
| **Busiest Day** | The day with the most play |
| **Most Played** | The game played the most |
| **Most Popular** | The game the most people played |
| **Week over Week** | Who increased their playtime the most compared with the week before |

### Last 30 Days

| Tile | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Completions** | Who finished the most games |
| **Library Growth** | Who added the most games |
| **Sessions Logged** | How many sessions were logged |
| **Streak** | The longest run of days in a row with a session |
| **Total Hours** | Everyone's hours added together |
| **Top Platform** | The platform played most |

### All Time

**Total Hours**, **Sessions**, **Streak** and **Completions**, across you and everyone you follow, since you all joined.

There are a few secrets hidden around FrogLog for you to find, and Highlights is one place to look.

### New From Friends

Under the highlights, **New From Friends** shows games the people you follow have started or played in the last 7 days, with their avatars. Use the arrows to scroll through. If you don't follow anyone, this shows **New from You** instead.
